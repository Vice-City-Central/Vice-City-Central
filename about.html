WHY THIS SITE EXISTS

Made by a fan, for fans.

Vice City Central is an independent fan project and a place to learn web development while celebrating the Grand Theft Auto series. Browse fan-made guides and character profiles, explore a few facts, and try the quiz.

This is not an official Rockstar Games website. News and game details can change, so check Rockstar's official channels for the latest announcements. Rumors and fan commentary are not official news.
