Fun Facts

A few quick facts about the worlds and characters in both games.

GRAND THEFT AUTO V

Three playable protagonists

GTA V follows Michael, Franklin, and Trevor. You can switch between them during much of the story.

GRAND THEFT AUTO V

A big place to explore

The game’s map includes Los Santos and the surrounding Blaine County, with city, desert, and coastal environments.

GRAND THEFT AUTO VI

Welcome to Leonida

GTA VI is set in the state of Leonida, which includes a new version of Vice City.

GRAND THEFT AUTO VI

Meet Lucia and Jason

Lucia and Jason are the central duo introduced in the game’s trailers.

Try These Activities

These are fan-made challenges for your next time playing.

GTA V CHALLENGE

Character switch challenge

Switch between Michael, Franklin, and Trevor, then make up a short photo story that connects all three characters.

GTA V CHALLENGE

Los Santos photo safari

Pick a theme—cars, coastline, or city views—and capture five different photos that fit it.

GTA VI CHALLENGE

Vice City sightseeing list

Explore the city and make a checklist of interesting places, views, or details you discover along the way.

GTA VI CHALLENGE

Lucia and Jason story snapshots

Take a few in-game photos that tell a mini story about the duo, using a beginning, a twist, and an ending.
