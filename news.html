Rockstar's current GTA VI page lists November 19, 2026 and PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It describes Vice City as part of Leonida and Jason and Lucia as the central duo.

TAKE-TWO — NOVEMBER 9, 2026

Take-Two said launch was ten days away

In its fiscal second-quarter results, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said GTA VI's launch was ten days away and described pre-orders as strong. The current game page continues to list November 19.

Source type: Dated statement by the publisher's CEO; not a review or guarantee.

OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT — JUNE 24, 2026

Pre-orders and launch platforms were announced

Rockstar and Take-Two announced pre-orders and identified PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the launch platforms. That release set November 19, 2026, replacing the May 26, 2026 date in Rockstar's earlier announcement. The US announcement listed $79.99 for the standard edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition; local prices can differ. It also said digital pre-loading would begin November 12.

Source type: Corporate press release from Take-Two.
